### Core Value Proposition
- **High-Velocity Scanning**: Sub-50ms barcode lookup, auto-refocus input, and tactile scan-to-cart animations.
- **Multi-Category Specialization**: Unified flat product architecture accommodating Fragile, Cold, Tech, Cleaning, and General inventory metadata.
- **Backend-Enforced RBAC**: Middleware-gated authorization for Admin, Inventory Manager, and Cashier tiers.
- **Atomic POS Transactions**: Complete checkout rollbacks on insufficient stock with flat 5% tax computation.
